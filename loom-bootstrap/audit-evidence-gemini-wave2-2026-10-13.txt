2. Linux Clipboard Verification (WSLg / X11):
   - Multi-target clipboard ownership (`loom_clipboard::write_clipboard`) on Linux is routed through `copypasta::ClipboardContext` which only writes plain text UTF-8 and drops HTML/JSON targets.
   - `xclip -selection clipboard -t text/html -o` exits with 1 (`Error: target text/html not available`).
   - Reading external X11 HTML selection authored by LibreOffice fails with `IoError("Unexpected Reply type: 299")` in `copypasta`.
   - Truthful finding: rich multi-target clipboard is Windows-only; on Linux it is plain text only.
